# Art direction and visual systems

Derive the direction from audience, purpose, medium and supplied brand rules. Name the desired mood in concrete design terms: editorial contrast, calm instructional clarity, playful geometry, technical precision. A vague request for premium is not a design system. When useful compare two genuinely different directions through small studies; do not add a concept-selection interview to a quick task.
Specify a shared canvas, margins/grid, spacing unit, type scale, palette roles, focal treatment and illustration language. Current native typography is measured English Arial, not arbitrary fonts. Enforce supplied brand colours when possible, but check text/background contrast and make supported alternatives explicit. Native palette tokens are background/foreground/muted/accent; explicit colours may supplement them deliberately.
Make hierarchy visible through proportion, placement, spacing and contrast. Let the meaning choose the layout. Use an open composition when cards add no information; negative space is useful. Avoid making every object equally prominent. Decoration cannot compensate for unclear content or unreadable text.
Create a small native specimen with title/body/value/shape samples when a complex project benefits from it; use edit_canvas or a document page and inspect actual output. Preserve a selected direction through revisions and across subjects. Do not regenerate a whole design to change one label. Review at realistic phone/projection size; inspect Canva font substitution and reflow separately after import. Read quality-review before final delivery.
For long native presentations compose_slide can establish an editable composition rather than a text-only page. Choose its kind from the actual relationship, adapt it and use composed charts or real provided image assets when meaningful. Establish focal contrast, a visible hierarchy and useful diagrammatic structure; avoid repetitive cards, unnecessary decoration and shrinking dense paragraphs. Inspect rendered pages at presentation size and keep detailed requirements in notes without losing mandatory visible copy.
