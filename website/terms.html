Service and current access

GraphicRouter is a prototype for planning and composing editable infographics and presentations, making focused revisions and exporting supported designs. It is currently free and offers no purchases. Access depends on ChatGPT plugin availability and account permissions. Public directory publication is still in preparation. Third-party account plans and usage limits apply separately.

Your content and responsibilities

You retain ownership of your own content. Submit only material you are entitled to use and authorize GraphicRouter and the connected providers to process it for the design actions you request. Do not include credentials, identity documents or unnecessary sensitive personal information. Use the service lawfully and follow the applicable OpenAI and Canva terms.

Review before use

Review facts, source links, spelling, numbers, layout and the actual exported or imported result before sharing or relying on a design. Generated content and previews can contain errors. Do not represent drafts, supplied claims or unverified features as independently verified evidence.

Versions, Canva and retention

GraphicRouter revisions create separate stored versions. A Canva import creates a Canva copy; manual Canva edits do not synchronize back. Imported-object fidelity must be checked in Canva. Saved designs expire from application access after 30 days from original creation; see the privacy notice for deletion status and scope. Keep your own copies of work you need to retain.

Prototype limits and changes

Features, supported formats, availability and usage limits may change. The current tool does not promise arbitrary file editing, lossless conversion or uninterrupted availability. If you stop using it, manage your Canva copies and authorization separately and contact support about application-held data.
